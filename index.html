Dear family & friends,

Six years ago we walked into East West College of Pharmacy knowing little more than the difference between a tablet and a capsule. Since then it has been practicals, viva after viva, case presentations, and a whole year of internship on the hospital wards.

On 21st November we graduate as Doctors of Pharmacy. It would mean a lot to have you there when we take the Pharmacist’s Oath and throw our caps in the air.

With love,

How to reach

  • Bus: get off at Anjana Nagar bus stop, the main stop for the campus. Bharath Nagara 1st Phase stop is also close, about a 600 m walk.
  • Metro: Jnana Bharathi station (Purple Line), then a cab, auto or the feeder bus towards Bharath Nagar.
  • Cab or auto: search “East West College of Pharmacy” in Uber, Ola or Rapido.
  • The auditorium is on the 6th floor. Please arrive by 9:45 AM.
